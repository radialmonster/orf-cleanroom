## S1. Verified
- S1.1 On four large-sensor CC0 files (ids 7267, 7269, 7801, 7803) the #11 parse gives tokens per pixel within 0.3% to 2.1% of an independent-bits null, so the ordinary code does not fit it; token correlations at lags 1 to 6 are small but nonzero and escapes (q >= 17) are far more frequent than the null gives. Scope: these four files only. On 13 files from three OM-1-family bodies (OM-1 ids 5283 to 5287; OM-1 Mark II 7262, 7263, 7264, 7265; OM-3 7796 to 7799) the excess over the null is lower at higher bits per pixel within each body (the trend is broad, not strictly monotonic at high bpp): +1.6% to +10.4% on OM-1, +5.8% to +11.8% on OM-1 Mark II and 0.0% to +5.2% on OM-3. The body sets the level: at 5220x3912 and 7.76 to 8.11 bpp it is +5.7% to +5.9% on OM-1 Mark II, +1.6% on OM-1 and 0.0% to +0.5% on OM-3. On ten further files in the id ranges named by the lead (OM-1 Mark II 7266 to 7270; OM-3 7800 to 7804) the per-file variation and the broad bpp trend hold, and the body offset remains at comparable bpp (at 8.45 bpp, +2.25% on OM-1 Mark II versus -0.33% on OM-3). The earlier four-file range uses its original 1,000,000-token prefix; the newer 1,500,000-token run gives +2.25% on 7267. All ten record Exif 0x8827 [200]; strict high-bpp monotonicity is not claimed. So closeness to the null is not a fixed body property, but the body is not irrelevant either. Scope: Partial verification of lead #106; ISO response and the scene/setting property are not tested. [F-null-bpp; 2026-10-20--radialmonster--large-sensor-k5-parse-near-bernoulli-null, self-verified; F-null-bpp-2]

- S1.2 **Initial-token split on the four large-sensor exemplars (shared unassigned cohort, not a V2 signature):** the first payload token on ids 7267, 7269, 7801 and 7803 is q=47 with suffix 0, not q=39; a bounded whole-payload parse counts q=47 exactly once and q=39 zero times on each. Each payload has one extra leading zero byte before the same `00 00 00 00 01` pattern as the 83 q=39 files. The cause of the extra byte and any assignment to V2 are not established. [F-q47]

- S1.3 **Shared unassigned candidate-family comparison:** on the four q47-prefix files, nine fixed candidate-family counts versus one measured-density Bernoulli draw reproduce for 131072-byte prefixes; predeclared 262144-byte prefixes use the same captures. Real Elias delta completes zero words on all four at both lengths, leaving the entire window unconsumed. The seed resets across files/windows, and p is re-estimated per window. One draw is descriptive and these counts do not identify a codec, framing, pixel correspondence or V2 membership. [F-family-null]

- S1.4 **Shared unassigned whole-payload comparison:** nine fixed candidate-family counts per stored site and a single full-payload measured-density Bernoulli control reproduce on three q47 files; predeclared 7803 is a separate previously studied extension. Real Elias delta completes zero words on all four, leaving the whole payload residual. Seed reuse induces dependence and one draw is descriptive; these counts do not identify a codec, framing, physical pixel correspondence or V2 membership. [F-family-whole]

- S1.5 **Shared unassigned token-index parity mean decomposition:** on 1M and predeclared 2M candidate-token prefixes of the two original OM-1 Mark II q47 files, the fixed between-parity mean component contributes far less than half the positive lag-2 minus lag-1 Pearson difference. On the two OM-3 files the difference is nonpositive and this criterion is not applicable. This only addresses the specified mean component; other parity structure, a physical mechanism, CFA and codec remain unestablished. [F-q-parity-lag]

These shared unassigned results do not establish V2 membership. Container facts that apply (compression tag 1 on every file; fixed-size payloads told by exact size, so compressed means variable size) are in [V0.md](V0.md) S1.

Shared terminal-bit evidence is recorded in [V1.md](V1.md) S1.7-S1.8 and G10 [F-tail, F-end]. It verifies counts and endpoint composition on a frozen unassigned cohort, not V2 membership or a padding rule; V2 decoding remains unspecified.

## S2. Unverified details and earlier inventories
- S2.2 The earlier statement that these files share a 31-bit escape is not established. Lead #37 found no q=30 escape in 11 CC0 files (escapes at q 17 to 29), and F-q39 found none in three further whole payloads. In those three, q=39 is the initial token and occurs exactly once, so the q=39 entries in the inventory below are that start token, not end-of-payload fill. Measured on 15 CC0 compressed files (tools/orf_tokens.py, first 1.5 to 3 million tokens; ids 1993, 1994, 1995, 1996, 3801, 6343, 8549, 8552 and the large-sensor ids 3573, 5284, 7265, 7267, 7269, 7801, 7803): escapes (q >= 17) fall at q 17 to 28, most at 17 to 21 and falling after; the rest are isolated (q 31 once, 32 twice; q 47 is the initial token of each of the four largest files, and q 39 is the initial token of each of the other eleven; see S1.2 and F-q47). There is no peak at q 30 or 31, and the distance at which the stream resynchronises after an escape (--escape-landing) does not single out a 31-bit word. So a 31-bit escape is not established by public measurement. [F-q39, F-q47; the 15-file inventory remains unverified]
