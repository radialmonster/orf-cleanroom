## S1. Verified
- S1.1 Parsing the payload with the k=5 token model (q zero bits, stop bit, 5-bit suffix) consumes the whole payload to within 4 bits on every file tested in full, 6 of 6; that is a statement about those six files, not a corpus-wide bound. A bounded parse of the 87-file unassigned cohort leaves 0-7 bits before the payload end (S1.8). The row period equals W where the row-start profile is sharp; busy bodies carry more than W tokens per row. [F-period, F-end]
- S1.2 On the four 5240-wide E-M1MarkII files (ids 1993, 1995, 1997, 1998), in the first 400 width-sized token blocks, cross-field association dominates at sequential lags 1 and 3 and same-field association at lag 2; the assumed-stride vertical lag-2 L-L MI is 0.017 to 0.018 bit on three prefixes and 0.0007 on id 1993. These are token-index offsets, not established physical rows. [F-em1] A separate run reproduces the selected prefix figures on 1995/1997 and extends their full four-field tables to whole payloads: lag-1 H-to-L falls from 0.0390/0.0394 to 0.0280/0.0281 bit. Smaller off-pattern cells are nonzero; within-token H/L MI is 0.0005 in the prefixes and 0.0007 whole, versus baselines printing 0.0000. This supports alternating association, not literal cross-/same-field exclusivity, exact independence, CFA identity or a decoder rule. [F-lags-full]
- S1.3 With the same parse, b4 of one token carries 0.95 to 0.98 of the baseline-adjusted information between H of that token and L of the next, on E-M1MarkII ids 1993 (5240 wide) and 1994 (10400 wide), 400-row prefix and whole payload. The amount of that information differs by file: about 0.05 bit on id 1993, 0.09 to 0.11 bit on id 1994. [F-b4b2]
- S1.4 With the S1.1 parse (k=5, stop bit 1, escapes parsed like any token), whole payloads, tokens parsed divided by payload width x height is 1.027 to 1.036 on four CC0 files from bodies not 5240 wide (E-P3 and E-PM1 4080x3040: 1.0271, 1.0287; E-M1 4640x3472: 1.0363; E-M10 Mark IV 5200x3904: 1.0287) and 1.155 on TG-7 (4040x3016, 1.1552), the busiest of the five by bits per pixel. Escapes (q >= 17) per file: 1606 to 25462 (TG-7 the most), 0.008% to 0.18% of tokens. Scope: Partial, item 2 of lead #29 on these five files only; the lead's calm 5240-wide figures are tested separately in S1.6, and the E-M1 value is slightly outside the lead's 4640-wide range (1.025 to 1.034). [F-tpp]
- S1.5 On three further whole CC0 payloads from bodies lead #37 did not use (E-M1MarkII 1993, OM-1 5283, OM-5MarkII 8549), the first five payload bytes are `00 00 00 00 01`, an initial q=39 token. Exactly one q=39 occurs in each payload, so only at the start; no q=30 occurs and non-initial escapes lie at q 17 to 28. Escape rate rises with bpp (13, 11074 and 24213 escapes at 6.151, 6.913 and 7.836 bpp), and the two escape-rich files peak at q=21. [F-q39]
- S1.6 Whole-payload tokens per pixel on the 5240-wide and 5220-wide bodies not covered by S1.4: 1.00130 on E-M1MarkII id 1993 (5240x3912, 6.151 bpp), 1.03952 on OM-5 Mark II id 8549 (5240x3912, 6.913 bpp) and 1.12756 on OM-1 id 5283 (5220x3912, 7.836 bpp).
